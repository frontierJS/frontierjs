The landscape

Nothing here was invented.

FrontierJS is an argument assembled out of other people's work — a hook pipeline from one, a policy language from another, a routing convention from a third, and a couple of cautionary tales about what happens when the seams are closed too tightly. This is the register of what it came from, and where each one stops.

The two numbers are self-assessed by the author, not measured: closeness is how literally similar the code and vocabulary are, influence is how much was actually taken. Treat them as a stated opinion with its reasoning attached — which is what the last two columns are for.

Bigger means more of whatever you pick. A cloud is a good way to see the shape of a field and a poor way to compare two numbers — so every value it hints at is printed exactly in the register below, driven by this same control.
